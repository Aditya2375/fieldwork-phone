Jarvis Phone

Jarvis Phone is a personal Android app. It takes a message I create on my phone and sends it as an email from my own Google account to one fixed email address that I choose.

To do this, the app uses the Gmail API with a single permission: gmail.send. It cannot read, modify, or delete email, and it cannot access anything else in the account.

The app runs no servers. Nothing is stored or processed anywhere other than on the phone itself.
