Legacy privacy notice

Archived project scope. This page describes the October 26, 2026 Gmail-send-only design. It is not the privacy notice for the current Jarvis voice-call app, and its description must not be read as a claim about that app.

Legacy design date: October 26, 2026. Archive clarification: November 4, 2026.

What the app does

This legacy Jarvis Phone design was a personal Android email-relay app. Its described function was sending a message from the operator's Google account to one chosen recipient.

Data collection

The legacy design did not include a separate personal-data collection service. The legacy notice described no analytics, advertising or tracking. This archive does not verify the behavior of any current app build.

Data storage

The legacy notice described no app-operated server or separate message store. Sent email is handled by Google Gmail and the chosen recipient; this notice does not claim that email exists only on the phone.

Data sharing

Sending an email transmits its content through Google Gmail to the chosen recipient. The legacy design described no additional sharing service.

Use of Google account data

The legacy design used Google sign-in and the Gmail API's gmail.send scope to send user-triggered email, rather than read, modify or delete inbox messages. This is an archived design description, not a claim about the current voice-call app.
