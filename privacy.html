Legacy privacy notice

Archived project scope. This page describes the October 26, 2026 Gmail-send-only design. It is not the privacy notice for the current Jarvis voice-call app, and its description must not be read as a claim about that app.

Legacy design date: October 26, 2026. Archive clarification: November 4, 2026.

What the app does

Jarvis Phone is a personal Android app. Its only function is to send a message I create on my phone as an email, from my own Google account, to one fixed recipient address that I choose.

Data collection

The legacy design did not include a separate personal-data collection service. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party components that gather information.

Data storage

The app runs no servers and stores no data on any server. Sent email is handled by Google Gmail and the chosen recipient; this notice does not claim that email exists only on the phone.

Data sharing

Sending an email transmits its content through Google Gmail to the chosen recipient. The legacy design described no additional sharing service.

Use of Google account data

The app signs in with Google and uses the Gmail API's gmail.send scope solely to send the email the user explicitly triggers. It does not read, modify, or delete email, and it does not use Google user data for any other purpose, including advertising or profiling.
