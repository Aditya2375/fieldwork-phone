Privacy Policy for Jarvis Phone

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What the app does

Jarvis Phone is a personal Android app. Its only function is to send a message I create on my phone as an email, from my own Google account, to one fixed recipient address that I choose.

Data collection

The app collects no personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party components that gather information.

Data storage

The app runs no servers and stores no data on any server. Messages exist only on the device until the moment they are sent as an email.

Data sharing

No data is shared with anyone. The only transmission the app ever makes is the email itself, delivered through the user's own Gmail account to the single fixed recipient address.

Use of Google account data

The app signs in with Google and uses the Gmail API's gmail.send scope solely to send the email the user explicitly triggers. It does not read, modify, or delete email, and it does not use Google user data for any other purpose, including advertising or profiling.
